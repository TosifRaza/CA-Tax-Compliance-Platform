# Service form review — working source map

This is a starting point for field-by-field professional review, not an approved specification. Portal flows, filing periods, applicant type and local jurisdiction change the questions and evidence required. Record the exact form version, source date, field, applicability, required/optional status and reviewer decision before publishing a configuration. Do not collect government portal passwords, OTPs or biometric data.

A structural validation pass is not proof that the questions or the legal rules match the portal. Existing application snapshots must remain intact when later configuration versions are prepared.

## West Bengal municipal trade licence: initial field inventory

The urban manual lists the following data. Its field inventory alone does not establish which fields are mandatory in every municipality; review the actual jurisdiction and portal branch before marking them required. Rural P&RD and Kolkata-specific paths must be assessed separately.

| Area | Candidate portal data | Review / minimization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applicant | Salutation, first/middle/last name, date of birth, gender, mobile, email and Aadhaar-related entry | Reuse verified profile information where appropriate. Confirm which fields are mandatory and whether the portal's Aadhaar step can be completed directly by the applicant; do not collect OTPs. |
| Establishment | Country, state, district, subdivision, authority type and name, borough, ward, police station, post office, address and communication address | Restrict this draft to West Bengal municipal cases; confirm authority-specific fields and ask for a separate communication address only when different. |
| Trade | Firm/company name, expected commencement, site rent or annual valuation, business category, trade/profession, requested licence duration, workshop and godown addresses | Collect conditional site details only if applicable. The manual identifies annual and application fees as portal-generated; do not ask clients to invent them. |
| Owner / authorised person | Ownership type, name, mobile, email and address | Confirm multiple-person requirements and add repeatable entries where required; the current flat form cannot represent an arbitrary list. |
| Supporting documents | Applicant identity proof and proof of occupancy | The cited municipal guide describes one identity proof and one occupancy proof; acceptable occupancy evidence varies with ownership/tenancy/other cases. Confirm current portal format, size and local requirements. |

Application date and age should be derived only where the chosen portal actually requires them. Do not declare the inventory complete or publish it until applicability, mandatory conditions and the current authority's portal have been reviewed.
